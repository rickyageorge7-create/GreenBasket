About GreenBasket Farm Produce

GreenBasket is a student website project that presents examples of agricultural products available in Liberia.

Our Project Purpose

To present local farm produce in a clear, organized and accessible website.

Our Focus

Showcase local produce, product descriptions, sample units and prices.

Our Audience

Households, market customers and people interested in local agricultural products.

Our Approach

Use connected HTML pages, navigation, images, tables and a sample inquiry form.
