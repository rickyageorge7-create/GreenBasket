About GreenBasket

Our Story

GreenBasket began as a small produce stall inspired by the farms around Monrovia. We imagined a friendly place where neighbors could find everyday local foods in one stop.

This is a fictional business for a beginner HTML class project. The details on this website are sample information.

Our Values

  • Offer fresh local produce
  • Keep prices fair and easy to understand
  • Support nearby farmers
